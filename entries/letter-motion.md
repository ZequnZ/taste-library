# Letter motion

- **Status:** confirmed collection material, not a confirmed universal preference.
- **Source:** the user’s own `/Users/zequnzhou/Desktop/Code/myspace/personal/mysite2/docs/idea-taste-preview.html`, specifically **Letter motion**. Original HTML preserved unchanged in its source project.
- **Original context:** an `Idea` navigation link alongside Blog and Shelf in a personal site. Fredoka 600, warm paper and umber, with individual letters animated on hover. The source’s varied-font and full-screen-transition studies are not included.
- **User reaction:** asked to add Letter motion and Dashed line to the collection, and preview them from the gallery. Saved from that explicit request; no individual motion is preferred yet.
- **Contents:** doodle shuffle, wave, jelly, hop, spread, wobble and grow. Original timings and staggered `cubic-bezier(.22,1.5,.5,1)` spring motion retained.
- **Category / family:** Motion & interaction; individual element. Related to `dashed-line` through the same Idea navigation family.
- **Applicability:** a playful, personal navigation or text-link accent. Test legibility and avoid continuous motion for usability-sensitive controls.
- **Dependencies:** embedded static Fredoka SemiBold. Modern CSS; reusable as a single copied HTML document.
- **Accessibility:** selector buttons and separate Replay buttons; real `aria-pressed` states; animation also available on keyboard focus; reduced-motion final states.

Ligatures are disabled to retain individually animated glyphs.
